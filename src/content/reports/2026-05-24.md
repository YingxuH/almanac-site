Models equipped with AlphaProof-level logic won't just "guess" the next token based on training data; they will be capable of formally proving that generated code is free of certain classes of bugs before execution, eventually leading to zero-shot formal verification for mission-critical software.

**3. Data Centers Hit 6% of US Electricity Consumption**
New figures indicate that data centers now account for a staggering 6% of total US electricity consumption, sparking organized political and environmental backlash.
*Why it matters (3-30 yr outlook):* The era of unconstrained, brute-force compute scaling is colliding with the physical grid. If regulatory or economic caps are placed on power usage, the AI tooling market will shift violently from prioritizing massive frontier models to hyper-optimized, task-specific Small Language Models (SLMs) and on-device execution, fundamentally changing the unit economics of AI coding agents.

**4. Electrobun 2.0 Pivots to Rust, Decouples from Bun**
In a major architectural shift, desktop app framework Electrobun 2.0 has been completely rewritten in Rust, intentionally decoupling its core from the Bun JavaScript runtime.
*Why it matters (3-30 yr outlook):* Despite the proliferation of AI-generated high-level code, the bedrock of software infrastructure continues to consolidate around memory-safe systems languages. Rust is establishing an impenetrable moat for performance-critical layers, reinforcing the widening bifurcation between "glue code" (written by AI) and "systems code" (written and maintained by highly specialized human engineers).
