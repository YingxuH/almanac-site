7. **IBM Accelerates Quantum Commercialization**: IBM has spun off the first pure-play quantum chip foundry (*Futurum Group*), arriving alongside a heavily debated $2 billion US government equity investment into quantum startups. This represents a structural hardware shift from academic research to commercial manufacturing.
